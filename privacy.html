Privacy Policy – Scholix

Developer: Tal Feldman
Last updated: June 29, 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how Scholix collects, uses, stores, and protects your data. By using the app and consenting on the consent screen, you agree to the terms outlined here. If you do not agree, please decline the consent prompt and exit the app.

About Scholix

Scholix is an educational app designed to help students in Israel access their grades, schedule, and attendance data using their Webtop credentials. The app is for personal, non-commercial use only and is developed independently by Tal Feldman.

Disclaimer

Important: Scholix is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Webtop, SmartSchool, the Ministry of Education in Israel, or any school or academic institution. The app accesses data only when users enter their own credentials.

Data We Collect

  • Credentials: Your Webtop username and password, stored only locally on your device using encryption.
  • Academic Data: Fetched from Webtop after login, including name, grades, attendance, and schedule.
  • Usage Data: Minimal in-app behavior data (e.g., last sync time) stored locally. No online tracking or transmission occurs.

How We Use the Data

  • To log in to Webtop and retrieve academic data at your request.
  • To store data locally on your device for offline access.
  • To improve the layout and performance of the app (locally, without analytics).

User Consent

Before using Scholix, you must read this Privacy Policy and accept it on the consent screen. If you do not accept, the app will close and no data will be collected or stored. You may withdraw your consent anytime by clearing app data or uninstalling the app.

Data Security

Scholix uses strong encryption to store your credentials and academic data locally. Your information is never sent to external servers. You are encouraged to use your device's built-in security tools (PIN, biometrics) for additional protection.

Third-Party Services and SDKs

No third-party SDKs are used. Scholix does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs (e.g., Google Analytics, Firebase), social media SDKs, or any trackers.

No data is shared with third parties. All functionality is self-contained and offline. All processing occurs locally on your device.

Children's Privacy and Families Policy

Scholix is intended for users aged 13 and above. It does not knowingly collect or store data from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child is using the app inappropriately, please contact us immediately.

The app is not marketed toward children and does not use the Families Policy API. It is the responsibility of users and guardians to ensure proper access and usage.

Your Rights

  • You may view, update, or delete your locally stored data at any time.
  • You may uninstall the app to permanently erase all collected information.
  • You may revoke access by clearing login data in the app settings.

Cookies

Scholix does not use cookies or session tracking. However, Webtop may use cookies in its own login interface, as governed by its separate privacy policy.

Policy Updates

This policy may be updated to reflect new features or regulatory requirements. You will be notified of any significant changes via an in-app notice, and the "Last updated" date will be revised.
