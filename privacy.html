Privacy Policy – Scholix

Developer: Tal Feldman
Last updated: June 29, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how Scholix collects, uses, and protects your data. By using this app, you agree to the practices described here.

Disclaimer

Scholix is an independently developed application and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Webtop or any educational institution. Scholix uses publicly accessible web interfaces to retrieve academic data for personal, non-commercial educational use.

Definitions

  • Account: Your user account within the Scholix app.
  • Application: Scholix mobile app.
  • Developer: Tal Feldman.
  • Device: Any device you use to access the app.
  • Personal Data: Information that identifies you, including credentials and academic info.
  • Service: Scholix app functionality.
  • Usage Data: Diagnostic data such as IP, device type, and app usage patterns.
  • You: The user of the Scholix app.

What We Collect

  • Credentials: Your Webtop username and password to access academic data. These are stored securely and locally on your device in encrypted form.
  • Personal Information: Name, email, grades, attendance, and schedule pulled from your Webtop account.
  • Usage Data: Device info, session logs, errors, and diagnostics.

How We Use Your Data

  • To provide you access to your academic information
  • To enable functionality like grade viewing, schedule management, and attendance
  • To store your preferences and session securely
  • To improve app performance and user experience

Data Security

We take data protection seriously. All sensitive data, including your login credentials, are stored securely using encrypted storage on your device. No personal data is sent to external servers or shared with third parties.

Data Retention

Your data remains on your device and is retained only as long as you keep using the app. You can clear all data at any time via the app settings.

Data Deletion

You can delete all personal data stored in the app by uninstalling it or by using the “Reset” option in settings. No external backup or copy is stored elsewhere.

Children’s Privacy

Scholix is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. If you are a parent and believe your child has used the app, please contact us immediately.

Third-Party Services

Scholix does not use third-party analytics or tracking tools. All functionality is fully offline and self-contained.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted within the app and on this page.
