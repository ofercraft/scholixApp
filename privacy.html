Privacy Policy – Scholix

Developer: Tal Feldman
Last updated: June 29, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how the Scholix app collects, uses, stores, protects, and discloses information when you use the app. Your use of the app signifies your acceptance of this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please uninstall the app and do not use it.

About Scholix

Scholix is a mobile application designed to allow students in Israel to view their grades, attendance, and class schedules by logging into their school portal (Webtop). The app is developed and maintained by an independent developer and is intended for **personal, educational, non-commercial use** only.

Disclaimer

Scholix is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or officially connected to Webtop, SmartSchool, the Israeli Ministry of Education, or any school or educational authority. All data retrieved by the application is done solely with user-provided credentials and for the user's personal access only. You are solely responsible for complying with any institutional or governmental policies regarding your use of this app.

Data We Collect

  • Login Credentials: Your Webtop username and password are required to retrieve academic data. These are stored securely and only on your device.
  • Academic Data: This includes your full name, class schedule, exam scores, grades, attendance records, and school-related messages. These are pulled directly from Webtop upon your login.
  • Usage Data: Anonymous diagnostic data such as screen views, crash logs, and session duration. These are stored locally only and not transmitted to any server.

How We Use the Data

  • To log in to the Webtop service using your credentials (only when explicitly initiated by you).
  • To retrieve and display your educational information in a clear, student-friendly interface.
  • To store your academic data for offline access, encrypted on your device.
  • To improve user experience, layout rendering, and app reliability based on local behavior analytics.

Data Storage and Encryption

Scholix does not upload or store your data on any remote servers. All sensitive data such as login information, retrieved grades, and schedule data are stored **locally** on your device using encryption techniques (AES-256 or similar).

The app does not use cloud sync or backup. If you uninstall the app, all data is deleted from your device. If your phone is lost, your credentials and data will not be recoverable unless stored elsewhere by you.

Third-Party Access

Scholix does not share, sell, rent, or otherwise transfer your personal information to any third party. It does not use advertising SDKs or third-party analytics tools like Firebase or Google Analytics.

However, because the app connects to Webtop / SmartSchool, their privacy policy applies to any data accessed through their system. You are advised to read and understand their privacy policy.

Data Retention

Your credentials and fetched data are retained as long as the app is installed or until you manually delete them via the "Reset" option in the app’s settings. Data is automatically deleted upon uninstallation.

User Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access all data stored on your device by the app
  • Delete all stored data at any time
  • Revoke consent for access or storage
  • Uninstall the app to stop all further data collection or usage

Children’s Privacy

This app is not directed at children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child is using the app without your consent, please uninstall the app from their device and contact us.

Legal Basis for Data Processing

Scholix processes your data solely on the basis of your informed consent. By entering your credentials, you authorize the app to act on your behalf to retrieve your academic data. No automatic background retrieval occurs without your interaction.

Security

All efforts have been made to secure your personal information against unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure. However, no system is fully immune to breaches. You are advised to keep your phone secure and update it regularly.

Cookies and Tracking

Scholix does not use cookies or tracking mechanisms of any kind. However, Webtop (SmartSchool) may use cookies as part of its login/session management, and these are governed by their own privacy policy.

Policy Changes

This privacy policy may be updated as needed. All users will be notified via an in-app message when material changes are made. The updated policy will always be posted at the same URL or within the app settings screen.

Compliance and Reporting

If you believe your data has been misused or improperly accessed, please contact the developer immediately. All issues will be investigated and addressed promptly.
