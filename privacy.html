Use of the app must comply with your school or institution's policies.

Definitions

  • Account: Your user account within the Scholix app.
  • Application: Scholix mobile app.
  • Developer: Tal Feldman.
  • Device: Any device you use to access the app.
  • Personal Data: Information that identifies you, including credentials and academic info.
  • Service: Scholix app functionality.
  • Usage Data: Diagnostic data such as IP, device type, and app usage patterns.
  • You: The user of the Scholix app.

What We Collect

  • Credentials: Your Webtop username and password to access academic data. These are stored securely and locally on your device in encrypted form.
  • Personal Information: Name, email, grades, attendance, and schedule pulled from your Webtop account.
  • Usage Data: Device info, session logs, errors, and diagnostics (stored locally only).

How We Use Your Data

  • To allow you to view and manage your academic information inside the app.
  • To store your preferences and session information securely on your device.
  • To improve the app’s performance and stability (no usage data is sent externally).

Data Security

All sensitive data, including login credentials, is stored securely using encrypted local storage. The app does not transmit your personal data to any remote server. You are responsible for keeping your device secure.

Data Retention

Your personal data is stored only on your device and remains until you delete it or uninstall the app. No cloud backups or third-party storage is used.

Data Deletion

You may delete all stored data at any time through the app’s settings or by uninstalling the app. We do not store any data externally and cannot recover deleted data.

Children’s Privacy

Scholix is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. If you believe your child is using this app inappropriately, please contact us immediately.

Third-Party Services

Scholix does not integrate any third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. It operates fully offline. However, since the app connects to Webtop, you should also review the SmartSchool Privacy Policy.

Cookies

This app does not use cookies. However, the Webtop platform you log into may use cookies, as governed by their own policy.

Legal Use and Responsibility

You are responsible for ensuring your use of Scholix complies with the terms of your educational institution. By using the app, you agree that the developer is not liable for any violations of third-party terms or data use restrictions.
